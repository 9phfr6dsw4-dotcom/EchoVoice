EchoVoice has a new Pearl Editorial design: warm ivory, deep teal, and a calmer space for your words.

- Top navigation for Generate, Voices, Recordings, and Settings, with the existing keyboard shortcuts.
- A paper-like script editor with voice and free-form delivery controls alongside it.
- Individual voice cards, refined recording controls, and consistent settings and dialogs.
- A sculpted teal E app icon, with the modern macOS asset catalog and Finder fallback.

Your voices, recordings, scripts, settings, and downloaded models carry over. Audio generation still runs locally on your Mac.

Install: download EchoVoice.zip, unzip it, drag EchoVoice into Applications, and choose Replace. Open EchoVoice yourself after replacing it. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Quick check: switch between all four pages; preview a saved voice; generate a short script; try New Script and Undo; play the recording and use Same Take, New Take, and Use Script.
