EchoVoice now speaks with Qwen3-TTS, replacing Breeze TTS 2.

- One voice model: Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base (8-bit). The Balanced / Best quality / Fastest choice in Settings is gone.
- Delivery style is removed. The model ignores style instructions when it copies a voice from a clip. Old recordings keep the style text they were saved with.
- Same Take on an older recording sounds different, because the voice model is different.

Your voices, recordings, scripts and settings carry over. The first time you generate audio, EchoVoice downloads the new voice model once (a few GB); after that it works offline. The old Breeze files are not deleted: remove the folders starting with `mlx-community_Breeze-TTS-2` from `~/Library/Application Support/EchoVoice/Models/mlx-audio` to free the space.

Install: download EchoVoice.zip, unzip it, drag EchoVoice into Applications, and choose Replace. Open EchoVoice yourself after replacing it. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Quick check: generate a short recording with one of your voices and listen to it.

Qwen3-TTS has its own license; check its model card before distributing or selling anything built on it.
