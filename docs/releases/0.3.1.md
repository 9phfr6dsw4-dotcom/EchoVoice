EchoVoice now uses the whole window in full screen.

- The pages grow with the window instead of staying in a narrow column, and the navigation bar lines up with the page.
- On Generate, the script editor grows to fill the window's height, and the voice column widens a little on large screens.
- At the regular window size, and in small windows, nothing changes.

Your voices, recordings, scripts, settings, and downloaded models carry over. Audio generation still runs locally on your Mac.

Install: download EchoVoice.zip, unzip it, drag EchoVoice into Applications, and choose Replace. Open EchoVoice yourself after replacing it. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Quick check: open EchoVoice at its regular size (it should look as before), then go full screen on Generate, Voices, Recordings, and Settings; the layout should spread out and the script editor should be taller. Resize back to the smallest window and check that every page still fits.
