## EchoVoice 0.6.0

**EchoVoice now updates itself.** It looks for a new version when it starts and every hour after that. When one is ready, it installs it and reopens by itself, once no voice is being made, a model is downloading or being removed, or a clip is being recorded or transcribed. Your voices, scripts, recordings and settings stay as they are.

- **Check for Updates…** is in EchoVoice's app menu and on the **Settings** page, which also has the automatic checks and installs and the time of the last check.
- Every update is signed, and EchoVoice refuses anything that isn't signed by its own key.
- EchoVoice now goes online to check GitHub for a new version of itself, as well as to download the voice model. Your voices, scripts and audio are never uploaded.

Nothing else changes: the voice models, recordings, voices and the Pearl Editorial look are as in 0.5.0.

### Install

Install this version by hand once: download `EchoVoice.zip`, unzip it, and drag EchoVoice into Applications, replacing the old copy. If macOS blocks it, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. Allow the microphone again if macOS asks. After that, new versions arrive on their own.

Requires macOS 26 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.

### Verify the download

`EchoVoice.zip.sha256` holds the checksum. In Terminal: `shasum -a 256 -c EchoVoice.zip.sha256`.
