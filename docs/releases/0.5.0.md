EchoVoice now offers Qwen3-TTS and Chatterbox Turbo, with a model picker on Generate and in Settings.

- Corrected Qwen's voice-reference processing to match its official speaker encoder: Slaney log-magnitude features with the expected window and padding. Reference conditioning is prepared once per recording and retains the original clip safely.
- Added Chatterbox Turbo for English voice cloning. It uses your saved voice clip without requiring a transcript. Use more than five seconds of speech; 8 to 25 seconds is recommended.
- Each recording remembers its model. Same Take and New Take reuse that model; select another model on Generate to compare engines.
- Downloads and removal apply to the selected model. Chatterbox's extra voice tokenizer is stored with EchoVoice's downloads and loaded locally, so it works offline after the initial download.
- Fixed Chatterbox's generation limit and cutoff reporting.

Your voices, recordings and settings carry over. Qwen stays selected after updating, and its existing download is reused. Earlier recordings still play; regenerating a Qwen recording can sound different because reference processing is corrected.

Qwen3-TTS weights are Apache-2.0; Chatterbox Turbo weights are MIT. Both permit commercial use subject to their license terms. Voice similarity and speed still need listening tests on your Mac.
